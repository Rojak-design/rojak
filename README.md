# Rojak

Rojak is a home screen that links to separate mini apps (modules): Money, Fitness, Habits and Books so far. It runs in your phone's browser and can be
installed on your home screen. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no build step. **All data stays on your phone.**

## Home screen
- **Weather** (top-right corner): an icon, the temperature and where you are, e.g. ⛈ 27° Punggol. Uses the phone's location (asked once), rounded to about 1 km; it isn't saved or backed up (only the last forecast and place name are kept on the phone, so it still shows for a while offline, faded after an hour). In Singapore the icon and place come from NEA's 2-hour forecast for the nearest of its ~47 areas (data.gov.sg); the temperature and the next 12 hours (icon, temperature, chance of rain) come from Open-Meteo, which also gives the icon elsewhere. Outside Singapore the place name (district or town, e.g. Shibuya) comes from BigDataCloud's free lookup (only the last one is kept on the phone, so there's no history of places; "Near you" if it can't be found). All are free and need no sign-up; only the rounded location is sent (to Open-Meteo, and outside Singapore to BigDataCloud — the NEA area is picked on the phone). Refreshes when you open the app and every 30 minutes. Tap it for the next 12 hours. If location isn't allowed it shows **📍 Weather** (tap for how to turn it on).
- **Water** (not a module — five bottles in a white card at the top): tap where you want the water to reach — the lower half of a bottle fills it halfway, the upper half fills it to the top. Bottles fill left to right; tapping the last bottle at the level it's already at empties it (to undo a mistap). The water is this month's hair colour; underneath it says e.g. "2½ of 5", and when all five are full the bottles bounce and a few sparkles pop. Each day starts at 0 (also when the app is left open past midnight); earlier days stay saved in the shared store under `mod:water` (`days: {date, bottles}`, bottles in halves) and are in the backup.
- **Money** card: what you spent today and your net total (the same total as on the Accounts screen: bank accounts minus the card bills due this month / pay cycle). Tap it to open the money tracker.
- **Fitness** card: this week's sessions by category, your latest record (a new best lift or fastest run) and your latest recovery score. Tap it to open Fitness.
- **Habits** card: **Today** — daily habits done today (and which are still to do); **At risk** — weekly and monthly habits that may not make it this week, month or pay cycle, with up to 4 listed (red when they can't any more): counts where what's still needed is about as many days as are left, limits already over or at 90% / on pace to go over, savings below the target, and loan payments still due in the last week of the month. Tap it to open Habits.
- **Books** card: a wooden bookshelf. The books you're reading stand on it first (with a red bookmark), then the books you've finished this year, newest first (up to 5, then "+2"…). It says "Empty shelf — tap to pick your next read" when you aren't reading anything and haven't finished a book this year. Underneath: **X books this month** and **Y books this year** (books finished, by finish date). Tap it to open Books.
- **Music, Scheduler**: placeholders for modules to be built later.
- **Memories** (not a module — a photo collage near the bottom): one day at a time as a scrapbook grid (some photos bigger than others), with the date at the top (e.g. "Sun 15 Nov"). Swipe left / right or tap ‹ › to move between days (it starts on today and doesn't go past it); tap the date to jump to any day, and **Back to today** returns. **+** lets you **choose photos** (several at once) or **take a photo**. Each photo goes on the day it was taken (read from the photo's details); photos without a date go on the day you're looking at. Photos are shrunk before saving (longest side 1600px, plus a small preview for the collage) and turned the right way up. Tap a photo to see it full screen (swipe or ‹ › through the day's photos) and to delete it. Saved in the same on-phone database (IndexedDB) under `photo:<id>` and `photos:index` (each with its date, time taken and when it was added) — kept apart from the modules' data, so they're not in the main backup or its restore / undo, and **Delete all data** leaves them.
- **Backup** (for the whole app, every module at once):
  - **Export backup**: one file with everything in Rojak (Money, Fitness…). Screenshots are never included, so the file stays small.
  - **Restore from backup**: shows what's in the file and what's on the phone for each module, and lets you tick which modules to bring back; the ticked ones are replaced, the others stay as they are. Older Money backup files (`money-backup-….json`) and earlier Rojak backups restore too (their screenshots are skipped).
  - **Undo restore / Undo delete**: a copy of everything (without screenshots) is kept just before each restore or delete, so the last one can be undone.
  - **Delete saved screenshots**: bank screenshots saved by earlier versions of Money stay on the phone until you delete them here (shown only when there are some).
  - **Back up photos**: a separate .zip of the Memories photos as plain JPEGs named by date (`2026-11-15_1.jpg`…) plus `photos.json` with their dates; it opens like a folder on a computer. **Restore photos** adds them back (photos already on the phone are skipped). **Delete all photos** (asks twice) is separate too. Shows how many photos there are and roughly how much space they use.
  - **Delete all data**: deletes every module's data (asks twice; can be undone, except screenshots).
  - A module screen left open while you restore or delete reloads with the new data instead of saving its old data over it.
- She and her cat peek over the top of the first card (the water bottles). Every month her hair colour changes and the cat is a different breed (Siamese, Scottish Fold, Orange tabby, Persian, Calico, Maine Coon, Russian Blue, Bengal, Sphynx, Black cat, Tuxedo, Ragdoll). Each time you open the app she waves and blinks while the cat swishes its tail; tap either one to see it again. With Reduce Motion turned on, they stay still. The browser tab shows her with this month's hair; the icon on your phone's home screen stays teal (phones don't let a web app change it after it's installed).

## Shared data store
The home screen and every module save through one store (`core/store.js`). Every entry (account, transaction,
reconciliation, recurring payment, project, trip, workout…) is saved with `savedAt` (when it was first saved) and
`updatedAt` (when it last changed), so modules can be compared later. Entries saved before Rojak were given
their best-guess date the first time the money tracker opened (when the account was created, or the entry's
own date). The money tracker's data stays where it always was, so earlier data and backups still load. Other modules are saved under `mod:<module>`. Screenshots are read for their numbers and then thrown away, never kept.

## Money module (money tracker)
- Multiple accounts (e.g. Savings, Spending) with a starting balance **as of a date**; entries dated before it (e.g. past trips added later) are kept for history and charts but don't change the balance
- **Transfers** between your own accounts (e.g. salary to a savings account): **⇄ Transfer** on the Accounts screen. A transfer is two linked entries (out of one account, into the other), never counted as spending or income; tap either one in History to edit or delete it. Paying a card bill is a transfer to the card. Regular transfers (e.g. part of your salary into savings every payday) can be set up under Plan → Recurring → **Transfer**; they're listed with what's due and can be logged in one tap.
- Credit cards: log purchases on a card, see what you owe, what you spent this month and last, the next due date and available credit. **Pay card bill** moves money from a bank account to the card (not counted as spending). Reconcile a card against **the bill to pay**: the bill due in that pay cycle (or month), less what you've paid, or the latest statement if none is due in it — the “amount to pay” your bank app shows. Switch to **Everything owed** to compare the card's whole balance instead. An adjustment for a bill is dated on its statement day, so it goes into that bill. Set the card's statement closing day and due day so bills are worked out per statement cycle. On the Accounts screen a card shows **what's due this month / pay cycle** (its bills due in it, less what you've paid), and the net total takes off only that, so the numbers match this period's finances; a bill due in the next pay cycle shows up when that cycle starts. Tap the card for everything owed on it and each statement. Each statement (on the card's screen and in the forecast) adds up: the amount owed you entered, if its "owed as of" date falls in that statement; spending logged on the card; planned entries; and recurring payments on the card due in it, even if not logged yet (past ones not logged are listed with **Log it**). Card payments (from the card's "owed as of" date on, including that day) pay off the oldest bill first — starting with the amount you entered when setting up the card — and anything extra carries on to the next bill, so once everything is paid each bill shows 0 left.
- Log expenses and income: amount, category, date, note (tap an entry to edit or delete it). Entries dated in the future (e.g. PayLater instalments logged ahead) are **planned**: they're tagged in History, don't affect today's balances, and are added to the forecast in the period (and card bill) they fall in.
- **Fill from a screenshot**: when adding a transaction, pick a screenshot (receipt, payment confirmation, ride…) and the app reads the amount paid and a note for what it was (well-known merchants like TADA, Grab, FairPrice also set the category). Other amounts and notes found are offered to tap; everything stays editable. For a list of transactions (e.g. a card's Recent transactions), the note is the shop name on the same row as the amount and the date is taken from the line under it; tapping another amount picks its name, date and (for well-known merchants) category. Reading happens on the phone.
- **Money back**: log refunds, or a friend paying you back for something you paid for, against the spending category it was for (e.g. Food). It goes into the account like income but is taken off that category, so category totals, the pie chart and the forecast show what you spent yourself.
- Categories for every transaction, with a category filter in History. History also has a pie chart of spending by category for this month (or pay cycle), with ‹ › to look at earlier ones (or later ones with planned entries); the transaction list below shows the same month / pay cycle as the chart (tap **Show all dates** to see everything). Spending linked to a trip is one slice per trip (✈️ KL…), whatever its category, so the categories show everyday spending; tap a trip slice to open the trip. Tap a category to see its transactions. Add, rename or remove categories under **Settings → Manage categories**.
- Daily reconciliation: attach a screenshot of your bank balance and the app **reads the balance from it** (on your phone, nothing is uploaded; the screenshot itself isn't kept) — it fills in the amount next to “Available/Current/Outstanding balance” and lets you tap another amount or type it if it's wrong — and see the difference from the app's balance. Optionally add an adjustment to make them match, and choose which category the adjustment belongs to.
- **Plan** tab:
  - **Recurring** – instalments, loan repayments, subscriptions, rent, salary… (amount, how often, when it ends). Shows what's due in the next 30 days and how many payments are left, and which account (or 💳 card) each is paid from; filter the list by account: tap any number of accounts (or All cards / All banks). **Reminders:** recurring payments that are due but not logged yet are listed at the top of Accounts (and Recurring) with **Log** (opens it filled in, on its date) and **Skip**. Turn on **Settings → Notify me when a recurring payment is due** to get a phone notification too: with Rojak installed on the home screen in Chrome on Android, the phone checks a few times a day in the background (not between 10pm and 8am) and notifies each due payment once. Other browsers can't check in the background, so there you see them when you open Rojak. Log a payment from it, or link a transaction to it when adding one.
  - **Loans** – a recurring payment can end "when a loan is paid off": enter what's still owed today, the repayment and the interest (already included in what's owed, or charged on what's left at a yearly rate). The app works out the payoff date, payments left, total to pay and interest, and lists loans with what's owed on the Accounts screen.
  - **Trips** – add past trips and future travel plans (country, dates, optional budget). Spending logged during a trip is linked to it automatically (or pick the trip for flights and hotels booked earlier). Money back from others (e.g. friends paying you back) can be linked to a trip — the app suggests the trip when it comes in after you're back — and is taken off the trip's spending, so each past trip shows your own share. Each past trip shows what it cost per day and booked ahead, by category; **Travel budgets from past trips** gives those per country, and future trips are estimated from them (same country if you've been, otherwise all trips). The forecast includes each trip on its dates, and keeps trip spending out of your everyday trends.
  - **Forecast** – filter by account (tap any number of accounts, or All cards / All banks) to forecast just those accounts' own spending, income, recurring payments and card bills (transfers between your accounts and trip estimates are left out then). The next 12 months of spending: recurring payments plus day-to-day spending (food, transport…) projected from your history, using the trend or the average of the last 3, 6 or 12 months. Tap a month for the breakdown. Switch to **Savings** to see how much you can save each month or pay cycle (expected income − spending), the total over 12 months, how that fits your savings projects, and where your bank balance is heading. If you use credit cards, each month or pay cycle also shows the card bills due in it (worked out from each card's statement cycle) and the cash leaving your bank accounts.
  - **Projects** – save for a house, wedding, etc.: list the expected costs, what's saved (typed in or an account's balance) and how much you save each month, and see how many months it will take and whether you'll make a target date. Each cost can have the month it's needed and be paid in monthly instalments (e.g. a sofa in 12 payments from July); the app then works out how much to save each month — either as payments come due (e.g. S$5,500 in Jan, then S$500 a month for instalments) or spread out ahead of big payments (e.g. S$1,833.34 a month until Jan, then S$500) — and updates it when costs or savings change. You can still choose a fixed amount instead. When you buy something, link the expense to the project's cost (or tap **Log a payment** on it): the project shows what was paid against the plan, counts instalments paid, and works out what's left. Each cost can list options — vendor / brand, a link to the product (tap **Open ↗**), price and note — and you can use an option's price as the plan.
- Pay cycles: set your payday under **Settings** (optionally moved to the Friday before when it falls on a weekend). The forecast, its chart and the category totals in History then use pay cycles (payday to the day before the next payday) instead of calendar months. Each pay cycle is named after the month its pay comes in (25 Nov – 24 Dec is November, also when the payday is moved to the Friday before a weekend). If your payday changes (e.g. a new job), use **Payday changing?** and enter the first payday on the new schedule: earlier cycles keep the old payday.
- Backup and restore are on the Rojak home screen (Money → Settings links there).
- Works offline, has light and dark mode

## Fitness module
Coral-coloured, so it's easy to tell apart from Money. It keeps the metrics side of training (Garmin and Whoop already log the workouts themselves). Four tabs:
- **Calendar** – a month calendar showing what you did each day by category: Strength, Weightlifting, CrossFit, Gymnastics, Easy run, Tempo run, Intervals, Long run, Race, Cycling, Swimming, Other. Each day's box is tinted by that day's recovery score (green 67%+, yellow 34–66%, red below, as in Whoop), and 🏆 marks a day you set a record (a lift's best est. 1RM so far, or a fastest run); tapping the day shows its recovery, HRV, sleep and the record. Below it, this week's and this month's sessions by category. A category counts once a day — two CrossFit entries on one day (e.g. its strength part and its workout, recorded separately in Garmin) are one CrossFit session in the totals. The calendar itself still shows every entry. Tap a day to change a session's category or name, add one, or delete one; **+** adds a session for today.
  - **📷 Import from Garmin**: screenshots of Garmin Connect's **All Activities** list (one screenshot covers several days; overlapping screenshots are fine), or the home screen's **Today's Activity** card (today's activities, no times; one already on the calendar that day with the same name or category is unticked). Each activity's name, date and time is read, and its category comes from the name: the category with the longest matching word wins ("Tempo run" → Tempo run, "Gymnastics WOD" → Gymnastics, "Crossfit Strength" → Strength; "6x800" → Intervals). Check the categories and tap Save; activities already on the calendar are unticked. Change the category for a name (on import or by tapping a day) and that name gets it next time too.
  - **Your links** (top of the calendar): save links you open often — e.g. the Google Drive spreadsheet where you track your workouts (in Drive: ⋮ → Share → Copy link). Tap one to open it; **Edit links** to add, rename, reorder or delete. Only web links (https://) are kept.
  - **Edit categories ›** (top of the calendar, and in the day and import screens — **Done** takes you back): add, rename, reorder, recolour or delete categories, change their icon (female versions: 🏋️‍♀️ 🤸‍♀️ 🏃‍♀️ 🚴‍♀️ 🏊‍♀️ …; for e.g. Hyrox 🛷 🎽 🏟️ 🏁; or type any emoji), and set the words that put an activity in each one.
- **Records** – your **fastest runs** (5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon: time, pace and date) and **max lifts** (log any weight × reps; each lift shows its heaviest set and best estimated 1-rep max, Epley: kg × (1 + reps/30)).
  - Tap a lift for its best by reps (1RM, 3RM, 5RM…), one chart of estimated 1RM (solid) and body weight (dashed, blue) over the same dates, both in kg on the same scale, and every entry with that day's body weight, recovery, HRV and sleep — to see how they line up. Saving a new best says so.
  - Tap a distance for every time saved and a chart of them (faster at the top). Times can be typed with dots: `22.21`, `1.51.49`.
  - **📷 Import Garmin Personal Records**: a screenshot of Garmin Connect's Personal Records → Running page reads the 5K, 10K, half and marathon times and dates. Each time is kept, so the history builds as your records come down; ones already saved are unticked.
  - Weights logged in the earlier version's workouts were added as lifts (the best set of each movement in each workout).
- **Body** – body weight, height and (optionally) body fat %, one entry per day. Shows the latest weight and the change since the entry before, BMI and body fat.
- **Recovery** – recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep (h:mm or hours), sleep quality (Whoop's sleep performance or Garmin's sleep score) and Body Battery (Garmin), one entry per day. The recovery score is green (67%+), yellow (34–66%) or red, as in Whoop.
  - **📷 Import from Whoop / Garmin**: Whoop recovery page (recovery %, HRV, resting heart rate, sleep performance) and Last Night's Sleep page (hours of sleep) — pick both together, or one now and the other later; Garmin home screen cards, At a Glance and the HRV / Sleep pages (resting heart rate, HRV, Body Battery, sleep score and hours of sleep (Duration) — the HRV Status page's **Overnight Avg** is last night's HRV and is what's used; the big number in its ring and the HRV card on the home screen / At a Glance are the 7-day average, which doesn't replace a last-night HRV). Numbers read go over that day's entry (the others stay). When Whoop and Garmin give different numbers for the same thing — in one import, or one app today and the other later — the **worse** one is used (lower recovery, HRV, sleep and Body Battery; higher resting heart rate), and both are shown under it.

Screenshots are read on the phone with the same bundled reader as Money (`money/ocr`) and never saved or uploaded; tap one in the check screen to see it bigger.

Saved in the shared store under `mod:fitness`, every entry with its own `date` (YYYY-MM-DD), so other modules (e.g. a habit tracker) can read it:
`categories` (`{id, name, icon, color, words}`, in the order shown), `activities` (`{date, time, name, catId, source}`), `nameCats` (`{'activity name': catId}`, categories picked for a name), `lifts` (`{date, name, weightKg, reps}`), `runRecords` (`{date, distance: 5k|10k|half|full, timeSec}`), `links` (`{date, name, url}`),
`bodyMeasurements` (`{date, weightKg, heightCm, bodyFatPct}`), `recoveryDays` (`{date, recoveryScore, hrvMs, restingHr, sleepHours, sleepScore, bodyBattery, sources}`). Empty numbers are saved as `null`. Workouts logged with the earlier version are kept in `workouts` / `workoutTypes` and shown on the calendar.

## Habits module
Violet, so it's easy to tell apart from Money and Fitness.
- **+** adds a habit: a name, how often (**Daily**, **X times per week** or **X times per month**) and a **start date** (today unless you pick a past or future date). Tap a habit's name to edit or delete it.
- Tick the round button for **done today**.
- **Daily and weekly habits** show a small month calendar (Mon–Sun): filled squares for days done, grey squares before the start date, today outlined. Tap a day to tick or untick it. Weekly habits get a green ✓ at the end of each week that met the target (and "2/3" for this week so far). Tap ‹ › or swipe the calendar to look at earlier months (back to the month the habit started).
- **Monthly habits** show Jan to Dec in one row of 12 squares: filled when that month's target was met (a number shows how many times so far when it wasn't), this month outlined, months before the start date grey. ‹ › switches year. Tap a month to tick or untick its days.
- A habit with a future start date shows **Starts on [date]** until then.
- An optional **end date** is a habit's last day (set it when adding or editing; **Clear** removes it). Until then the habit shows "until [date]". After it, the habit moves to **🏁 Finished habits** (folded away at the bottom, latest first), keeping its calendar, final and best streak and Done %. Nothing after the end date counts, the last week or month counts if its target was met part-way but isn't held against you if not, and finished habits no longer appear on the home screen.
- Each habit shows its **streak** (days, weeks or months in a row that met the target; today / this week / this month only counts once it's met, and isn't a miss until it's over), its **best** streak and **Done %** (periods met ÷ periods finished since the start date). Weekly and monthly habits also show this week's or month's progress, e.g. "2 of 3 this week".
- Weeks run Monday to Sunday; months are calendar months. When the start date falls part-way through a week or month, that first week or month counts if you met the target but isn't held against you if you didn't.

- **Manual or Linked**: when adding a habit, choose **Manual** (you tick it) or **Linked** (checked automatically from another module's data — Habits only reads it, never changes it). For a linked habit pick the module, what to measure, which items count, the target, **week** (Mon–Sun) or **month**, and the start date; leave the name empty for one like "Run 3× a week". Linked habits use the same calendar or 12-square designs (days that counted are filled in) and show progress toward the target, e.g. "2 of 3 runs this week". They can't be ticked by hand.
  - **Fitness – sessions in chosen categories**: every session on the Fitness calendar in the categories you tick (**🏃‍♀️ Runs** ticks Easy run, Tempo, Intervals, Long run and Race), e.g. "Run at least 3 times a week".
  - **Money – amount saved**: how much the bank accounts you tick went up: their total at the end of the week or month minus their total at the end of the one before (worked out like the Accounts screen; entries dated after today don't count yet). Card bill payments and money moved to other accounts lower it, money moved in raises it, moves between two ticked accounts cancel out, and spending on a credit card only counts once the bill is paid. With a payday set in Money, a month is a pay cycle named after the month the pay comes in (25 Nov – 24 Dec is November, even when the payday is moved to the Friday before a weekend). Each week or month is measured whole, from the accounts' total at the end of the day before it began, and judged when it ends: until then it shows what's saved so far and how much you can still spend (or how far short you are). A week or month that began before one of the accounts was added to Money isn't counted (its balance before then isn't known, e.g. a starting balance that already includes this pay cycle's salary); tracking starts with the next one. Tap a month to see its amount and dates.
  - **Money – credit card spending (at most)**: purchases on the cards you tick, less money back on them; paying the card bill doesn't count. Per week, month / pay cycle, or the card's **statement period** (one card). A limit is met when the week or month ends at or under it and missed as soon as it goes over (✕ / red outline).
  - **Money – spending in chosen categories (at most)**: e.g. Food under S$600 a month; spending from every account and card less money back, as in History's pie chart.
  - **Money – pay off a loan by a target date**: pick a loan from Plan → Recurring and the month to finish by. Each month's target is what's left ÷ the months to go (with the loan's interest rate), so paying extra lowers later targets; payments count when logged against the loan. Shows what's left and when you'd finish at the current repayment.
  - **Money – save for a savings project**: for projects whose savings are an account's balance: how much that account went up each month (measured and judged like Amount saved) against the project's fixed monthly amount, or (other plans) what's still to pay less what's saved, spread evenly over the months until its costs are due.
  - **Money – no-spend days**: days with no day-to-day spending on any account or card (recurring bills, card bill payments and own transfers don't count against you), counted once the day is over.
  - **Money – days you logged entries or reconciled**: e.g. log spending on 5 days a week, or reconcile once a week.
  - **Water – bottles of water a day**: from the bottles on the home screen, e.g. "Drink at least 5 bottles a day" (target up to 5, in halves). Checked over each **day**: a day is done (filled in on the calendar) when you reached the target; it counts with the daily habits on the home screen's Habits card.
  - **Books – books finished**: books marked Finished in Books, counted on their finish date, per **week** or **month**, e.g. "Finish at least 2 books a month". The days you finished a book are filled in on the habit's calendar; changing a book's finish date in Books moves it to that week or month.
  - New rules (for future modules) are added in `habits/rules.js`: one entry each, described at the top of that file.

Saved in the shared store under `mod:habits`, every entry with a date: `habits` (`{name, kind: 'manual'|'linked', freq: daily|weekly|monthly, target, date (start date), end (optional last day), rule (linked: {id, …choices such as cats or accounts})}`), `checks` (`{habitId, date}`, one per day ticked).

## Books module
Wood-brown, to suit the bookshelf.
- **+** searches **Open Library** (free, no sign-up) by title or author as you type; results show their covers. Tap one to add it (pick its status, dates, stars and notes first). Only what you type in the search is sent to Open Library. A book already in your list opens instead of being added twice.
- **Can't find it? Type it in yourself**: title, author, and **Take a photo** / **Choose a photo** of the cover. You can also put your own photo on a book from Open Library.
- Each book has a **status** (Want to read / Reading / Finished), **start date**, **finish date**, an optional **rating** (1–5 stars; tap the chosen star again to clear it) and **notes**. Choosing Reading fills in today as the start date if it's empty.
- **Finishing a book** (the Finished status, or **Mark as finished** on a book you're reading) asks for the day you finished it (today unless you change it) and lets you tap 1–5 big stars ("Loved it"…). The finish date can't be in the future or before the start date.
- Books you are reading or have finished show 5 stars on the list: tap one to rate the book right there (tap the chosen star again to clear it). **Mark as finished** on a book you are reading opens the finish date and stars; **Start reading** on a Want to read book moves it to Reading (started today).
- **Reading timeline**: the Books screen shows **Currently reading** at the top, then your finished books as a vertical timeline, newest at the top, by year (e.g. **2026** · 4 books) and then month (**November** · 3). A month's books sit side by side as covers, with their stars under them. **Want to read** is folded away at the bottom (tap it to open).
- Tap any book or cover to see its details: a big cover, the author, status, stars (tap to rate), when you started and finished it and how many days it took, and your notes. **Edit details** changes or deletes it; **Mark as finished** / **Start reading** are there too.

- **Habits**: add a Linked habit with module **Books** (e.g. "Finish at least 2 books a month"); see the Habits module below.

Saved in the shared store under `mod:books`, every entry with a date: `books` (`{title, author, status: want|reading|finished, start, finish, rating (1–5 or null), notes, date (the day it was added), olKey, coverId (Open Library), hasCover}`). Cover pictures are kept in the same on-phone database as small JPEGs (longest side 400px) under `bookcover:<id>` — covers picked from Open Library are downloaded there so they show offline, and your photos are shrunk first. Like Memories photos they aren't in the backup file (it stays small); after a restore, Open Library covers show from Open Library again, and books whose own photo isn't on the phone get a plain coloured cover with their title.

## Publish with GitHub Pages
1. On GitHub, open this repository → **Settings** → **Pages**.
2. Under **Build and deployment**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch**.
3. Choose the branch (e.g. `main`) and folder **/ (root)**, then tap **Save**.
4. Wait 1–2 minutes and refresh. Your link appears at the top: `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repository-name>/`

## Install on your phone
- **iPhone (Safari):** open the link → Share button → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the link → ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.

## Important: back up regularly
Your data is stored only in this browser on this phone. If you delete the app, clear browser data,
or lose your phone, the data is gone. Use **Export backup** on the Rojak home screen and save the file somewhere safe.
On iPhone, the home-screen app and Safari keep separate data — use the home-screen app consistently.

## Files
- `index.html` – the Rojak home screen
- `core/home.js`, `core/home.css` – home screen
- `core/store.js` – the shared data store (IndexedDB), entry dates, backup export / restore / undo / delete for the whole app
- `core/avatar.js` – her drawing, its monthly hair colours and her waving hand
- `core/cat.js` – the cat and its monthly breeds
- `core/water.js` – the water bottles on the home screen
- `core/photostore.js`, `core/memories.js` – the Memories photos (storage, dates, shrinking, photo backup) and the collage
- `core/weather.js` – the weather in the top-right corner (location, NEA and Open-Meteo forecasts, icons)
- `money/` – the money tracker: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` (accounts, transactions, categories, reconciliation, settings), `plan.js` (recurring payments, savings projects, trips and the forecast)
- `fitness/` – the fitness module: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` (calendar and categories, records, body measurements, recovery), `import.js` (reading screenshots)
- `books/` – the books module: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` (Open Library search, adding and editing books, covers, finishing and stars)
- `habits/` – the habits module: `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js` (the Habits screen: list, calendars, forms), `engine.js` (periods, progress, streaks and "at risk", shared with the home screen), `rules.js` (linked-habit rules that read other modules' data)
- `money/ocr/` – Tesseract.js (Apache 2.0, licences included) for reading screenshots (Money balances and receipts, Fitness imports); loaded only when you pick a screenshot
- `manifest.json`, `sw.js`, `rojak-icon*`, `rojak-apple-touch-icon.png` – make it installable and work offline (`icon-v2.svg`, `apple-touch-icon-v2.png` are the money tracker's own tab icon)

When you change any file, bump `VERSION` in `sw.js` and the matching `?v=` numbers in `index.html`, `money/index.html`, `fitness/index.html`, `habits/index.html` and `books/index.html` (keep them all the same) so phones pick up the update with all files matching.
